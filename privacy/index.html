Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Real Life RPG ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Store

Real Life RPG does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

Your gameplay progress, including character information, skill progress, activities, achievements, quests, and in-game rewards, is stored locally on your device.

The App does not currently operate its own account system or cloud synchronization service.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Locally stored gameplay information is used to provide the App's features, save your progress, and display your character and skill progression.

We do not currently use this information for advertising, behavioral tracking, or analytics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not currently sell your personal information or share your locally stored gameplay progress with third parties through the App.

Your device's operating system and backup settings may affect how app data is backed up or restored. Those processes are governed by the relevant platform provider.

4. Notifications

The App may offer notifications for reminders, activities, quests, or other features. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings and the App's notification controls.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data remains stored on your device until it is deleted by the App, reset by you, or removed when you uninstall the App. Device backups may retain copies according to your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Real Life RPG is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The App does not require users to submit personal information to create an account.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact
